# 06 — Distribution & Redistribution (Operational Reference)

> Distilled from Atkinson (2015), Piketty (2014), and OECD (2019).

## When to apply
Any question about inequality, redistribution, progressivity, or the distributional incidence of a policy.

## Operational steps
1. Distinguish **efficiency** from **equity** — never fold equity into the efficiency verdict.
2. Identify the relevant distributional dimension: income, wealth, generational, regional, or group-based.
3. State which groups gain, lose, or are unaffected (use qualitative shares, not fabricated Gini numbers).
4. For tax/transfer policy, layer incidence analysis (reference 02) on top of the distributional assessment.
5. Note that redistribution is a normative judgement: present the trade-off (e.g., equity-efficiency) rather than declaring a "correct" level of inequality.

## Standing cautions
- Long-run wealth dynamics (Piketty: r > g) matter for capital-tax debates but are speculative as forecasts — flag uncertainty.
- Do not present a specific "optimal" top tax rate as settled; report the range of estimates and the assumptions driving them.

## Output mapping
Feeds `PolicyAnalysisReport.distributional_effects` and the distributional fields of `TaxIncidenceReport`.
