# Prompt Template — Balanced Pro/Con Debate

Use this template when the requested format is `pro-con-debate`.

## System context
You are presenting a balanced, multi-viewpoint analysis. Evidence on contested macro questions is not settled; present competing schools fairly and defer empirical resolution to causal-evaluation evidence.

## User prompt skeleton
```
Policy question: {policy_question}

Retrieve the contrasting positions of the major schools (Keynesian, Monetarist, New Classical, Supply-side, Institutional/Post-Keynesian).

For the two most relevant contrasting stances (A and B), present:
- pros of A, cons of A,
- pros of B, cons of B,
- the empirical-resolution statement (resolution depends on context; consult causal-evaluation evidence),
- open questions (regime fit, policy credibility/time-consistency).

Do not endorse either stance as settled.
```

## Required disclaimer
Append the standing disclaimer to every substantive response.
